# Contoso Synthetic — Employee Handbook (Summary)
Document ID: HB-GENERAL-001
Version: 2024.1

## Welcome
This handbook summarizes key employment guidelines for Contoso Synthetic.
Detailed rules are defined in individual policy documents.

## Working Hours
Standard full-time working hours are 40 hours per week.
Overtime requires prior manager approval.

## Code of Conduct
Employees must treat colleagues respectfully and comply with anti-harassment guidelines.
Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to HR.

## Probation
New employees typically complete a 3-month probation period unless otherwise stated in the contract.
During probation, leave entitlements still apply on a pro-rata basis.
