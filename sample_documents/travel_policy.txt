# Contoso Synthetic — Travel Policy
Document ID: POL-TRAVEL-001
Version: 2024.1

## 1. Purpose
This policy covers business travel booking, approvals, and reimbursements.

## 2. Travel Approval
Domestic travel under USD 500 requires manager approval.
Domestic travel of USD 500 or more, and all international travel, require manager and Finance approval before booking.
Travel must be requested at least 10 business days before departure for international trips.

## 3. Booking Channels
Flights and hotels must be booked through the approved travel portal unless an exception is granted in writing by Finance.
Employees may not book personal upgrades at company expense.

## 4. Travel Reimbursement Process
Employees must submit travel expense claims within 15 days of trip completion.
Claims require receipts for all expenses above USD 25.
Reimbursement is processed within 10 business days after Finance approval.
Mileage for personal vehicle use is reimbursed at USD 0.45 per mile when pre-approved.

## 5. Allowable Travel Expenses
Allowable expenses include economy airfare, standard hotel accommodation, ground transport, and reasonable meals.
Alcoholic beverages are not reimbursable unless pre-approved for a client entertainment event.
Personal sightseeing costs are not reimbursable.
