# Contoso Synthetic — Expense Policy
Document ID: POL-EXPENSE-001
Version: 2024.2

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines expense categories, approval thresholds, and submission rules.

## 2. Approval Thresholds
Expenses under USD 100 may be approved by the employee's direct manager.
Expenses of USD 100 or more require manager and Finance approval.
Expenses of USD 1,000 or more additionally require department head approval.

## 3. Expenses Requiring Pre-Approval
The following expenses require written pre-approval before purchase:
- Client gifts above USD 50
- Software subscriptions
- Training courses above USD 200
- Team offsites

## 4. Submission Rules
Expense reports must be submitted monthly, no later than the 5th business day of the following month.
Original or digital receipts are mandatory for expenses above USD 25.
Missing receipts may result in rejection of the claim.

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Items
The company does not reimburse:
- Personal commuting
- Traffic fines
- Pet care
- Home internet upgrades not required for work
