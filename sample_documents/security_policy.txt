# Contoso Synthetic — Security Policy
Document ID: POL-SEC-001
Version: 2024.1

## 1. Incident Reporting
Security incidents must be reported to security@contoso-synthetic.example within 1 hour of discovery.
Examples include suspected phishing, lost devices, and unauthorized access.

## 2. Access Control
Access to systems is granted on least-privilege principles.
Access reviews occur quarterly.

## 3. Device Loss
Lost or stolen company devices must be reported immediately so remote wipe can be initiated.
Employees are responsible for safeguarding devices in public places.
