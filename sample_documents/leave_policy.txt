# Contoso Synthetic — Leave Policy
Document ID: POL-LEAVE-001
Version: 2024.1
Effective Date: 2024-01-01

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines annual leave, sick leave, and leave carry-forward rules for Contoso Synthetic employees.

## 2. Annual Leave Entitlement
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Part-time employees receive annual leave on a pro-rata basis according to contracted hours.
New joiners receive leave entitlement from their start date, calculated pro-rata for the remaining calendar year.

## 3. Leave Carry Forward
Employees may carry forward up to 10 days of unused annual leave into the next calendar year.
Carried-forward leave must be used by 31 March of the following year.
Any carried-forward leave unused after 31 March will expire and cannot be encashed, except as required by local law.

## 4. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
Sick leave of more than 3 consecutive working days requires a medical certificate.
Unused sick leave does not carry forward.

## 5. Leave Request Process
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 7 days in advance for planned annual leave.
Managers must approve or reject requests within 3 working days.
Emergency leave may be requested with shorter notice and will be reviewed case by case.

## 6. Public Holidays
Public holidays are in addition to annual leave and are published annually by HR.
Working on a public holiday requires prior written approval and is compensated per the overtime policy.
