## Leave Carry Forward
Employees may carry forward up to 15 days of unused annual leave into the next calendar year.
This legacy draft conflicts with POL-LEAVE-001 which states a 10-day carry-forward limit.
Do not treat this document as the authoritative current policy without HR confirmation.
