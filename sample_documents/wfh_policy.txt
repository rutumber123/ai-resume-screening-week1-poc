# Contoso Synthetic — Work From Home Policy
Document ID: POL-WFH-001
Version: 2024.1

## 1. Eligibility
Employees in roles designated as hybrid or remote-eligible may work from home up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote roles are defined in the employment contract and are not limited to 3 days.

## 2. Core Hours
Employees working from home must be available during core hours of 10:00–16:00 local time on working days.
Meetings involving customers should prefer camera-on participation unless connectivity issues occur.

## 3. Equipment
The company provides a laptop for eligible employees.
Home office stipends of USD 200 per year are available upon submission of receipts for ergonomic equipment.
VPN must be used when accessing internal systems from home.

## 4. Security Expectations
Employees must lock devices when unattended and must not share company credentials.
Printed confidential documents taken home must be secured and shredded when no longer needed.
